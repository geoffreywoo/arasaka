Organization & authority

A cartridge crosses a counter. A facility changes administrator. A family requests another conversation. Each action passes through people with different authority. Arasaka brings manufacturing, protection, financial relationships, and research into one group without treating their decisions as interchangeable.

01

Group Secretariat

Tokyo sets group direction under restored leadership. The Secretariat retains executive mandates and the distinctions between a group decision and the permissions held by an individual business. Its records appear in both continuity and regional succession reviews.

02

Regional Counsel

Counsel examines the scope of an instruction. It does not make a technical observation into a legal permission, or a family relationship into an archive entitlement. When a question crosses businesses, counsel retains the wording that each department received.

03

Financial Relationships

Relationship teams establish who may give an instruction concerning an account or an asset. They retain agreements, succession packets, and verified correspondent identities. Financial custody does not by itself confer control of a facility gate or a personality construct.

04

Regional Security & Archive Operations

Security records movement and implements authorized access. Archive Operations retains construct references and their associated instructions. Both depend on an identified authorizing office; neither should have to guess what another division intended.
