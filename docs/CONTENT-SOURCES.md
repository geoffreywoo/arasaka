## Platform Profiles

Relic: a neural biochip associated with personality engrams. Commercial interaction with a stored personality and experimental transfer into a biological host are distinct applications.

Mikoshi: Arasaka's infrastructure for retaining and managing digital personality constructs, not a handheld consumer product. Soulkiller produces a construct; Mikoshi retains an archive; Relic carries a construct into a neural interface. The diagram explains roles, not an exhaustive topology or real implementation procedure. Isolation and access descriptions are corporate design extrapolations, not a validated security architecture.

Shingen: TKI-20, Arasaka, smart submachine gun. Yukimura: HJKE-11, Arasaka, smart pistol. Their identities and classifications follow the referenced setting. Shared manufacturing language and operating context are project copy. No unsupported fire rate, damage, capacity, dimensions, pricing, ballistics, or fabrication/deployment instructions are supplied.

## Speculative Aftermath

The project chooses Saburo Arasaka's restoration, informed by The Devil ending branch, as its premise. It does not designate a canonical sequel or claim every ending restores him. The website does not assign a precise future year.

The renewed corporate mandate, three-business information architecture, service categories, institutional succession language, headquarters appearance, regional directory descriptions, and post-2077 research direction are original creative additions. Tokyo and Night City are established setting locations; the directory is illustrative and does not list real addresses, staff, phone numbers, email accounts, or service coverage commitments.

All seven new images are generated reconstructions, not screenshots, extracted game models, product-certified photographs, or exact official silhouettes. See [asset attribution](../assets/ATTRIBUTION.md).

## Editorial Rules

- Keep public corporate copy direct; disclose the fiction in the footer, structured data, and repository documentation.
- Do not claim actual revenue, market value, employee counts, investment returns, response times, safety guarantees, recruitment, sales, or service availability.
- Add numeric specifications only with an identified source, edition/build, and appropriate fictional context.
- Preserve source names without implying affiliation or authorization.
- Japanese translations preserve the same distinctions; localization corrections are welcome.
