# Imperial Network: World Bible

## Premise And Boundaries

A public interface built by an empire that controls security, capital, and identity. The homepage is corporate; the archive is optional. Discoveries change the interpretation of an earlier statement rather than revealing a hidden game.

**Referenced setting:** Arasaka; Saburo Arasaka; Tokyo; Night City; Relic, Mikoshi, Soulkiller, TKI-20 Shingen, HJKE-11 Yukimura; the broad roles of security, banking, and technology. See [primary-source boundaries](CONTENT-SOURCES.md).

**Chosen branch, not canonical future:** Saburo's restored leadership after 2077. No fixed subsequent year, universal ending, revenue, workforce count, or real-world service is asserted.

**Original additions:** all offices and reporting relationships below, depicted facilities, public commercial copy, internal procedures, case participants, policies, and correspondence. Reconstructed images are not official depictions.

## Threads 2 And 3: Territory And Capital / NC-1102

Relative to transfer. There is no fixed future year and no implied chronological ordering against NC-0417.

| Day | Reference | Action | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | AS / 1102 / 01, revision 02 | Financial Relationships accepts the asset schedule and retained records | Contractor appointments sit outside the asset transfer; handwritten roster correction retained |
| 1 | TA / 1102 / 02, revision 01 | Cargo entrance resumes under the retained movement instruction | Maintenance team waits outside; no current gate instruction; cabinet key returned without a request |
| 7 | IR / 1102 / 03, revision 03 | Group review closes and responsibility is assigned | Return to work is not confirmed; correspondence and pedestrian exception remain |

**Public account:** "Regional transition complete." This means the institution controls the transferred facility and distribution has resumed. It does not mean everyone's access has been renewed.

**Discovery:** A gate report narrows that completion statement. An executive closure then narrows it again: a responsible office has been named, but the people are still not accounted for as returned. No record proves a threat, fraud, guilt, or deliberate punishment.

Do not invent a death, disappearance, violence, worker return, or a secretly solved case. The unease is the precise boundary of what each department can record.
