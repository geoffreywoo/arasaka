Product overview

Continuity for leadership, knowledge, and institutional memory.

Relic converts high-value knowledge into a governed continuity asset. Capture systems, integrity controls, private custody, and succession workflows operate as one accountable environment rather than a collection of disconnected tools.

01

Identity capture

High-fidelity capture with provenance, consent, and operator authority recorded at source.

02

Continuity archive

Encrypted, versioned archives held inside client-controlled custody boundaries.

03

Succession rehearsal

Controlled simulations test decision continuity before leadership transitions become urgent.

System architecture

A complete operating environment.

Hardware, software, custody, and governance are designed as one product boundary.

    Acquisition hardware and provenance controls.

  2. 02Integrity kernel

  3. 03Custody vault

  4. 04Governance console

    Consent, release, and succession policy.
