Mikoshi

An archive beyond the individual.

Platform profile

Protected infrastructure for the storage and management of digital personality constructs.

Mikoshi is Arasaka's engram archive environment, not a consumer device. It provides the protected digital infrastructure in which personality constructs can be retained and managed, separate from the biochip that carries a construct.

01

03

Platform relationship
