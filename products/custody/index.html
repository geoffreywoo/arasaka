Product overview

Custody infrastructure for assets that cannot be exposed to ordinary markets.

The Institutional Custody Network unifies protected storage, confidential settlement, and board-governed authority. It is designed for strategic capital, intellectual property, and mandate structures where privacy and proof must coexist.

01

Protected custody

Segregated vaults hold capital instruments, keys, patents, and authority records.

02

Confidential settlement

Counterparties settle without disclosing intent beyond the required governance boundary.

03

Mandate governance

Multi-party approvals and immutable receipts govern every material movement.

System in operation

Every movement crosses a governed threshold.

Custody sites separate intake, holding, settlement, and disclosure into physically and cryptographically distinct zones. Material movement requires a defined quorum and produces a permanent receipt.
