Product overview

A protected environment that moves with the people and assets inside it.

Autonomous Perimeter Systems combine sensing, coordinated machines, route assurance, and named human command. The system is designed to detect, classify, and contain risk while preserving explicit civilian and operational boundaries.

01

Sensor fusion

Facility, vehicle, aerial, and identity signals combine into one operating picture.

02

Coordinated response

Autonomous units reposition, escort, and contain under policy-defined limits.

03

Human command

Named operators authorize consequential actions and retain immediate override.

System architecture

A complete operating environment.

Hardware, software, custody, and governance are designed as one product boundary.

  1. 01Sensing layer

    Distributed detection and identity context.

  2. 02Coordination core

    Formation, route, and resource planning.

  3. 03Response units

    Purpose-built ground and aerial platforms.

  4. 04Command ledger

    Authority, exclusions, and action receipts.

Deployment

Controlled introduction. Measurable handover.
